Plot Point 1

The Hunt For Malnor

Recommended Level: 3

Overview:

Opener for Campaign 1.

Idea: Mathias Malnor, leader of the Alterations Department is set to be executed publicly, but the execution is not completed when the display is attacked and Malnor escapes. The breakout attempt creates chaos in the city center, as mercenaries attack from all sides. In the battle, players assist the city guards in taking down the mercenaries. After the area is cleared, an escort for a local Lord spots the players and brings them to Lord Balen Varros. Varros hires the players to form a group and assist in tracking down Malnor.

Key People, Places, and Things

Location: The City of Zephyros
People: Mathias Malnor, Solvik, Balen Varros, Vox Winterlow
Groups: Alterations Department, Eye of Zephyros, Hand of Zephyros, Will of Zephyros

Storyline Elements

  • Mathias Malnor has been captured, tried, and sentenced to be put to death on display. News of the underground Alterations Department's activities in the city of Zephyros spreads fast. Much of the city, horrified by the department's actions, have gathered at the city center to spectate the execution.
  • Campaign starts separated, following in player in what they do in the time leading up to the execution
  • The king of Zephyros, Vox Winterlow, addresses the crowd at the city center, detailing the crimes that Malnor committed.
  • Right before the execution is completed, a series of explosions disrupts the scene and Malnor broken out. Masked figures surround city center and begin attacking into the crowd
  • Players, alongside the city guard and Solvik, fight off against the masked figures until they are eventually defeated.
  • Solvik, spots the players assisting in defeating the masked figures and messages each of them to meet her in a nearby alley
  • The players meet each other for the first time, then they are introduced to Solvik, who mysteriously leads them to an isolated home in the High District. The home belongs to Balen Varros, the old lord who captured Malnor and turned him in.

Background

The Alterations Department is a trafficking ring that has been running underground in Zephyros for decades, mostly staying hidden from the eyes of citizens and law enforcement alike. After losing his son, Pavren Varros, to the department, Lord Balen Varros set out to find and bring down the notorious ring. After 4 years of searching, Balen tracks down the group's leader, Mathias Malnor. Varros discovered that Malnor was searching for Nullstone, a rare magical crystal capable of separating a soul from the body. Varros managed to track Malnor's supplier, and thus Malnor. The two fight with Varros being the eventual victor. Balen Varros turns Malnor in to the authorities of Zephyros, alongside the evidence needed to warrant his execution. In their conflict, Malnor managed to injure Varros right before he was defeated, severing Varro's leg. Due to his lack of trust in the authority's competence when it comes to tracking Malnor, Varros sends his escort, Solvik, to attend the execution in his place, as his disability does not allow him the freedom to attend himself.

Concluding The Adventure

The adventure is concluded once the party defeats Malnor. A public bounty will be placed on Malnor by the government, as well as a bounty placed by Lord Varros. If the players present information pertaining to Malnor's capture to law enforcement, they will be awarded by the city and lost Varros' favor. On the other hand, going to kill Malnor themselves would award the party favor to Varros, but they lose out on the greater bounty placed by the city.
